# Why now

## Oversight is a duty

Regulators now expect boards to **oversee** cyber risk the way they oversee financial risk.

- Disclosure windows are measured in days
- One named executive is accountable
- Reporting must be backed by evidence

# The five questions

## Questions for management

1. Who owns cyber risk, by name?
2. What are our top three risks, in business terms?
3. How fast can we detect and contain?
4. What did we test, and what failed?
5. Where are we under-invested?
